About our College

ABOUT OUR COLLEGE GCET JAMMU : Government College of Engineering and Technology, Jammu (GCET Jammu) is a premier public engineering institution established in 1994 in Chak Bhalwal, approved by the AICTE, affiliated with the University of Jammu, and granted Autonomous Status for the 2024–25 session. Its Vision is to emerge as a center of excellence in engineering education and research, producing globally competent professionals with strong ethical values to foster sustainable technological development. Its Mission focuses on providing student-centric technical education, collaborating with industry to enhance research and innovation, and instilling leadership skills to address global challenges. Accredited by the NBA for all five core B.Tech disciplines (Computer Science, ECE, Electrical, Mechanical, and Civil), the institution admits students primarily via the JKCET exam and features a highly functional campus with modern labs, placement cells, and comprehensive student amenities.
